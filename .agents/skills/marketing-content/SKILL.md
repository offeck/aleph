# Marketing Content

## Ground rules

1. **Positioning** comes from marketing/25 (competitive landscape — the moat is STL/CTL styling)
   + planning/42 (locked public-copy scope: search, carousels, controls, curation, bounded
   analytics — AI chat stays OUT of public copy) + marketing/117 (launch playbook), grounded in
   planning/01 (vision). (marketing/08 is the older GTM doc, pending supersede by 117.) Net:
   AI discovery — visual search + shoppable carousels + shop-the-look — for fashion/home Shopify stores.
2. **Pricing claims** only from financials/31. Current model: Free Preview
   (50 products) → Mini $10/mo → Starter $29/mo → Growth $99/mo → Pro
   $199/mo → private >10k, via Shopify billing.
3. **Public website scope:** doc 42 supersedes the old website-copy guardrail. The
   launch-facing site may promise Smart Search, SBI/STL/CTL carousels, CTR + bounded
   revenue analytics, carousel design customization, and result editing. Do **not**
   promise AI chat, shopper personalization, A/B testing, enterprise/agency dashboards,
   or bounding-box editing unless a later decision record unlocks them. **Smart Search**
   follows planning/42's caveats: fast hybrid search, **English-first and lexical-first
   at launch** (semantic as the engine warms) — never imply always-on real-time AI
   semantic search or multilingual search.
4. **Language:** merchant benefits, not ML jargon. "Shoppers instantly find similar
   items", never "1280-dim visual embeddings". The name is **Recomento** (planning/34).
5. **Audience register** per marketing/08: small/medium fashion & home merchants and
   the agencies serving them — busy, non-technical, conversion-focused.

## Output

- Strategy-grade pieces (positioning docs, launch plans) → numbered doc in marketing/
  via the new-doc skill.
- Reusable assets (email templates, listing copy) → plain files under marketing/,
  listed in marketing/README.md.
