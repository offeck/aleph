## Reading the numbers

- Impressions ↑ + installs flat → listing conversion problem (screenshots/copy/rating), not a traffic problem.
- Installs spike after a post → attribute it in `channels.md` Outcome (with the post link), not here.
- Uninstall rate >30% of installs → product/expectation mismatch; read the recent reviews.
- CWS data is weekly-noisy at this scale — judge experiments after 3–4 weeks, never after days.
- One listing change at a time (except the initial overhaul).
